Hermes Investor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Hermes Investor ("the app") is a personal finance tool operated by Ali Noerie ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

What we access

  • Bank account information (balances, transactions) for accounts you explicitly authorise via the Enable Banking PSD2 Open Banking API.
  • The app is read-only: it never initiates payments, never stores your banking credentials, and never shares your data with third parties.

How we use it

  • To display your account balances and transactions in a consolidated dashboard.
  • To categorise spend and produce personal finance summaries for the account holder only.

Where data is stored

Data is stored locally on the device you use to run Hermes Investor. We do not maintain servers that receive or store your banking data. The Enable Banking aggregator acts as a regulated intermediary under PSD2; their privacy practices apply to data they temporarily process to relay API calls to your bank.

Data retention

You can revoke access at any time by deleting the linked bank account from the Enable Banking Control Panel. We will delete the locally cached copy of your transactions within 24 hours of receiving such a request.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, rectify, and erase any personal data we hold. Contact: alinoerie@gmail.com.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Hermes Investor is a non-commercial personal finance tool. Enable Banking Oy (the data intermediary) is registered as an Account Information Service Provider under PSD2, regulated by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA).
